HORIZON BILLING AND FINANCE REQUIREMENTS
Read-only observation report
Generated: 2026-10-01

Scope
-----
Inspected without selecting subscription options, entering card data, submitting a payment,
submitting a refund, generating an invoice, or downloading/exporting data.

Observed access boundaries
--------------------------
- The shared role can open the subscription-management screen.
- The shared role is denied access to Accounts Receivable and card-processor configuration.
- Export/download and tax-report routes were not opened to prevent sensitive-data download.
- The replacement must enforce finance-specific authorization independently of general
  authenticated access and report authoring.

Subscription management
-----------------------
Observed capabilities:
- Show subscription records by user.
- Show signup and expiry context.
- Choose no subscription change or an annual extension.
- Open monthly-payment editing.
- Continue to a separate card-information step.

Requirements:
- Treat selection as a draft only; no renewal/change occurs until a clear final confirmation.
- Calculate pricing, discounts, credits, taxes, and effective date server-side.
- Show a review screen before external payment collection.
- Use hosted payment fields/checkout from a PCI-compliant processor; never accept, store,
  log, or expose raw payment-card or bank-account data.
- Persist provider token/reference, payment status, amount/currency, invoice reference, and
  idempotency key only after verified processor confirmation.
- Handle success, decline, cancellation, timeout, duplicate click, webhook retry, and
  reconciliation explicitly.
- Maintain subscription status history and notify authorized users before expiry, after
  renewal, and on payment failure.

Invoices, credits, receivables, and taxes
-----------------------------------------
- Support immutable invoices, invoice revisions, credit notes, payment allocations, and a
  subscription/credit ledger.
- Keep invoice states: draft, issued, delivered, partially paid, paid, overdue, void,
  written off, and refunded.
- Restrict receivables and financial dashboards to finance roles.
- Provide aging, balances, customer/work-order linkage, collection status, and controlled
  export only to authorized users.
- Support tax configuration with jurisdiction, rate/version, effective dates, exemptions,
  rounding, and invoice-time snapshots.
- Require explicit confirmation, audit logging, and short-lived protected links for any
  financial report/export/download.

Discount administration
-----------------------
The profile inventory exposed invoice-discount administration with create/edit actions.

Requirements:
- Named discount rules with effective dates and active/inactive state.
- Fixed/percentage calculation, eligibility criteria, stacking/exclusivity policy, and
  maximum use constraints.
- Server-side calculation and audit trail for every applied discount.
- Historical invoice discounts must remain immutable after issue.

Refund request workflow
-----------------------
Observed form concepts:
- Category: annual subscription, monthly subscription, or prepaid credits.
- Most-recent purchase date.
- Requested subscription/credit end date.
- Reason and required acknowledgements.

Requirements:
1. Validate required fields both client-side and server-side.
2. Create a pending refund request, never an immediate refund.
3. Record requester, category, supporting reason, relevant purchase references, requested
   end date, and acknowledgement versions.
4. Apply refund-window/policy rules and route exceptions to authorized review.
5. Require finance approval/rejection with reviewer identity, decision reason, and audit
   timestamp.
6. Execute a provider refund only after approval; reconcile webhook confirmation.
7. Create a matching immutable ledger reversal/credit note and notify the requester.
8. Prevent duplicate requests/refunds through idempotency and status locking.

Audit and security
------------------
- Log finance navigation, quote calculation, subscription selections, confirmation,
  payment-provider events, invoice changes, export generation, refund decisions, and
  ledger movements.
- Do not log card data, authentication tokens, full account identifiers, or unnecessary
  personal/financial data.
- Use least-privilege roles, step-up authentication for high-value/refund/export actions,
  secure secrets storage, encrypted data, and monitoring/reconciliation alerts.
