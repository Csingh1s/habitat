Global navigation requirements
------------------------------
The authenticated application provides these primary navigation items:
- Home: open the work-order dashboard.
- Scheduler: open the schedule or begin an inspection-booking workflow. This is a
  potentially state-changing entry point and should require authorization.
- Book Inspection: begin an inspection/work-order creation workflow; require
  authorization and confirmation before creating data.
- Profile: open company, inspector, report, integration, and work-order configuration.
- Order: buy credits, subscribe, or renew a subscription; requires explicit confirmation.
- Help: open contextual help.
- Logout: end the authenticated session; requires confirmation.

Schedule: Day View
------------------
Purpose: show a selected calendar day and support read-only navigation among schedule
views.

Observed requirements:
- Display the selected weekday and date.
- Provide Month View, Daily Map, and Week View selectors.
- Provide Previous Day, Today, and Next Day controls.
- Provide three adjacent monthly mini-calendars with selectable dates and previous/next
  month navigation.
- Provide Morning, Day, and Evening display segments.
- Provide a legend/help affordance explaining schedule colors, letters, and codes.
- Show the inspector/business schedule area for the selected date.

Schedule: Month View
--------------------
Purpose: provide a whole-month schedule grid and calendar-period navigation.

Observed requirements:
- Display a month heading and a grid covering every day in the selected month.
- Provide previous-month and next-month navigation.
- Provide This Week and Today controls.
- Provide month and year selectors, including historic and future periods.
- Permit drill-down from a day in the month grid to that date's Day View.
- Display schedule content in the context of the selected inspector/business.

Schedule: Daily Map
-------------------
Purpose: present mappable inspections for the selected date.

Observed requirements:
- Display the selected date and its scheduled-inspection context.
- Render scheduled inspection locations on a map when at least one valid mappable address
  is available.
- When no inspections exist or none have mappable addresses, present a clear,
  informational no-data explanation without creating or changing schedule data.
- Do not expose map data outside the authenticated user's authorization.

Schedule: Week View
-------------------
Purpose: show a Monday-through-Sunday weekly schedule and workload summary.

Observed requirements:
- Display the week-start heading with previous-week and next-week navigation.
- Provide Month View, This Week, and Today controls.
- Display one selectable Day View link for each day of the week.
- Display a weekly schedule grid in the inspector/business context.
- Display aggregate workload metrics: total inspections, average inspections per day, and
  total appointments for the week.
- Where authorized, schedule entries may show appointment time, estimated duration,
  location, and client information. Access to this personal/operational data must be
  role-controlled.

Home dashboard
--------------
Purpose: find and filter work orders, expose operational summary actions, and signal
migration availability.

Observed requirements:
- Provide a Find text field for client, address, or report-number search and a Go action.
- Provide inspector and work-order-status filters.
- Support these observed status categories: all, works in progress, booked, in progress,
  completed, and cancelled.
- Provide Reset to clear filtering.
- Show the number of work orders matching the active filters and an empty-state message
  when none match.
- Provide Business at a Glance and Accounts Receivable actions. Their permissions and
  financial data must be role-controlled.
- Provide a password-change action that requires reauthentication/secure confirmation.
- Display subscription validity information only to authorized account users.
- Display a Horizon-to-Spectora retirement/migration notice and a route to migration
  settings. Migration must be explicit, reviewed, and consent-based.

Cross-cutting requirements
--------------------------
- Enforce authentication and role-based authorization for every module and sensitive
  record.
- Log navigation and all state-changing events with actor, timestamp, before/after state,
  and outcome.
- Clearly distinguish read-only navigation from actions that create, edit, publish,
  integrate, export, purchase, migrate, refund, or delete.
- Require explicit confirmation immediately before irreversible or financial actions.
- Avoid placing client, address, billing, report, or integration credential data in
  unprotected URLs, logs, or exports.
- Provide accessible labels for fields and controls and useful empty/error states.
- Preserve the selected inspector, date, and filter context while changing compatible
  schedule views.
